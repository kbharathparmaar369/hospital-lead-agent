How 24/7 Voice Scheduling Works

Powered by LiveKit Realtime Voice AI, Cal.com scheduling, and automatic email confirmations.

01

Click the Phone Widget

Tap the glowing phone icon in the bottom-right corner or click "Call AI Receptionist". No app installation or login needed.

02

Speak Naturally

State your reason for visit, name, and preferred day (e.g. "tomorrow morning" or "next Monday"). The AI parses natural speech instantly.

03

Live Slot Lookup

The agent queries our real-time Cal.com calendar and reads out 2-3 genuine available consultation times for your chosen day.

04

Instant Confirmation

Pick your slot and the appointment is secured in Cal.com, logged to hospital systems, and a confirmation email is dispatched immediately.
